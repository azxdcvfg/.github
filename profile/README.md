### Core Capabilities (Voxler)

* **3D Volumetric & Isosurface Modeling:** Transforms scattered 3D data (`XYZC` coordinates) into continuous 3D grids using algorithms like Inverse Distance, Kriging, and Natural Neighbor, displaying results as volume renders, orthogonal/oblique slices, or 3D isosurfaces.
* **Well & Borehole Visualization:** Models downhole paths, lithology intervals, geophysical logs, and contaminant plumes in true 3D space.
* **Point Cloud & LiDAR Rendering:** Imports and filters large 3D point clouds and topographic DEM surfaces to combine surface terrain with subsurface geology.
* **Interactive Presentation & Export:** Supports interactive camera fly-throughs, video animation exports, and 3D PDF or CAD/GIS exports.
